A FAIRER FUTURE OF WORK

India has millions of skilled workers. What is missing is a system that connects their skills to the right opportunities — fairly, intelligently, and sustainably. UNIVO brings customers, skilled workers, cooperatives, and government into one connected ecosystem. Every job becomes more than a transaction. It creates intelligence — improving trust, skills, opportunity, wellbeing, and the future of work.
